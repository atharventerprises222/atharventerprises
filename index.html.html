Netafim

Precision Irrigation & Smart Farming

Netafim is a global precision-irrigation company founded in 1965 in Israel. It pioneered modern drip irrigation and focuses on helping growers use water and other resources more efficiently. Its solutions cover agriculture from the water source to the plant root zone.

Its product portfolio includes drippers and driplines, sprinklers and micro-sprinklers, filters, valves, pipes, connectors and accessories, water meters, water tanks and digital farming solutions. Netafim also provides crop-specific agronomic knowledge and smart irrigation tools.

Signet Industries

Micro-Irrigation, Pipes & Irrigation Products

Signet Industries Ltd. is an Indian company associated with polymer products and agricultural irrigation solutions. Its irrigation business includes systems and equipment used for efficient water application in agriculture.

Its irrigation offering includes drip and sprinkler irrigation systems, pipes, fittings and related agricultural products. Signet's irrigation solutions are used across different crops and farming applications, with technical and design support for field requirements.

McCain Foods

Potato Processing & Contract Farming

McCain Foods is a global producer of frozen French fries and potato products. McCain Foods (India) is a wholly owned subsidiary of McCain Foods Limited and has been involved in agriculture research and development and the frozen-food market in India since 1998.

McCain works with local farmers and focuses on smart and sustainable potato farming. Its agricultural work includes improving water-use efficiency, optimizing fertilizer use and encouraging better farming practices. Potatoes that meet the required quality and processing characteristics can be supplied into the processing chain.

Irrigation Products

Drip, Sprinkler & Irrigation Equipment

Atharv Enterprises provides irrigation solutions and equipment for different crops and field requirements, with a focus on efficient water application and reliable farm irrigation.

💧

Drip Irrigation System

Drip irrigation delivers water slowly and directly near the root zone of plants. It is suitable for vegetables, orchards, cotton, sugarcane, chilli, fruits and many other crops.

  • Drip laterals and drippers
  • Main lines and sub-main pipes
  • Filters and pressure-control equipment
  • Connectors, valves and fittings
  • Fertigation support
🌧️

Sprinkler Irrigation System

Sprinkler irrigation distributes water over the field in the form of droplets, similar to rainfall. It can be useful for field crops, vegetables, nurseries and other suitable agricultural applications.

  • Sprinklers and micro-sprinklers
  • Risers and sprinkler stands
  • HDPE/PVC and other irrigation pipes
  • Valves and connectors
  • Pressure and flow management
⚙️

Other Irrigation Equipment

We also deal in supporting irrigation components used to install, operate and maintain irrigation systems according to field needs.

  • Screen, disc and other irrigation filters
  • Control valves and non-return valves
  • Pipe fittings, elbows, tees and connectors
  • Pressure regulators and gauges
  • Fertigation and Venturi equipment
  • Water meters and irrigation accessories
🌱

Fertigation Equipment

Fertigation equipment helps apply dissolved fertilizers through an irrigation system. Proper filtration, pressure and dosage management are important for reliable operation.

  • Venturi injectors
  • Fertilizer tanks
  • Injection accessories
  • Filters and control components
🔧

Pipes, Fittings & Accessories

A complete irrigation installation requires suitable pipes, connectors and control accessories. We can help farmers identify components according to their irrigation layout and field requirements.

  • Mainline and sub-main pipes
  • Take-off connectors and grommets
  • End caps and flushing accessories
  • Clamps, joints and repair fittings
📐

System Planning & Support

Choosing an irrigation system depends on crop, soil, field size, water source, available pressure and required flow. We help farmers understand the equipment needed for their application.

  • Crop-wise irrigation guidance
  • Basic system component selection
  • Water-saving irrigation solutions
  • Maintenance and replacement guidance
